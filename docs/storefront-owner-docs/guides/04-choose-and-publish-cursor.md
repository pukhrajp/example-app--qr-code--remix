### Choose and publish a cursor

This guide explains how to pick a cursor and push it live to your storefront.

### Key idea: Preview vs Published

- **Preview (selected)**: what you're currently looking at inside the app
- **Published (active)**: what customers see on the storefront

Your storefront only updates when you click **Save & Publish**.

### Steps

1. Open **Shopify Admin -> Apps -> Custom Cursor**.
2. In the left panel (Gallery), click a cursor.
3. In the right panel (Cursor Preview), move your mouse inside the preview area to see it in action.
4. Adjust settings if needed (size / enable status).
5. Click **Save & Publish**.

### Reset to default

If you want to remove the custom cursor selection:

1. Click **Reset to default**.
2. Click **Save & Publish**.

### Notes about timing

For performance, the storefront may cache cursor settings for about **5 minutes**.

If you publish a change and don't see it immediately:

- Wait a few minutes, or
- See `reference/01-troubleshooting.md` (clear cache / verify embed)

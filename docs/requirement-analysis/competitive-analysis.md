## 🎓 Lessons from Kaching

### What We Can Learn

#### 1. Simplicity Wins
- Users love "one-click setup"
- Fast time-to-value is critical
- Don't overcomplicate MVP

#### 2. Free Works
- 100% free model attracts users
- Our free tier must be competitive
- Premium tier for differentiation

#### 3. Variety Matters
- 100+ cursors is a key selling point
- Users want choices
- Must match or exceed in gallery

#### 4. Theme Compatibility Critical
- "Works with latest themes" is highlighted
- Must work reliably with all themes
- Testing is essential

#### 5. Quality Over Quantity
- Perfect 5.0 rating with only 29 reviews
- Better to have fewer happy users
- Focus on product quality first
